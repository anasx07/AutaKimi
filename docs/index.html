Getting Started

AutaKimi is completely local-first and runs natively on Windows 10/11 and Android devices without emulators or cloud accounts.

Windows Desktop

  1. Download AutaKimi.exe from the Releases Page.
  2. Run the installer or portable build directly.
  3. Pick English or Arabic (RTL) and select your studio theme.

Android Mobile

  1. Download app-arm64-v8a-release.apk (51 MB, recommended for 64-bit phones).
  2. Install the APK on your Android device.
  3. Pair with Desktop over LAN Wi-Fi or restore a .json.gz backup.

Multi-Repo Extensions

AutaKimi ships as an empty shell and hosts no media. You install sandboxed extensions from your chosen repositories with full feature parity across Desktop and Android.

  • Repo Color & Version Isolation: Add AutaKimi and Keiyoushi repositories side by side. Each repo keeps an isolated catalog, distinct badge color, and independent version checks.
  • Source Badges Everywhere: Cards in History, Library, and Downloads automatically display the owning extension badge so duplicate series names never blur together.
  • One-Click Updates & Bulk Refresh: Check available updates directly inside Extensions on both Windows and Android.

Player, Reader & Comfort

Designed for long reading and binge-watching sessions without visual fatigue or clutter.

  • Floating PiP & Mini Player: Shrink playback into an in-app corner player while browsing or pop it into an always-on-top desktop window.
  • Night Eye-Care Filter: Apply a warm sepia tint and custom dimming overlay directly inside the reader without changing Windows or Android system brightness.
  • Smart Downloads: Automatically caches the next 3 chapters as you read and cleans up completed ones to protect storage.
  • Local Wrapped & Discord Presence: View weekly watch/read hours, streaks, and top genres locally, or toggle optional Discord Rich Presence in Settings.

Local Wi-Fi Sync & Backups

Keep reading state, library entries, and history synchronized between Windows and Android with zero third-party cloud servers.

LAN Wi-Fi Discovery

When Desktop and Android are on the same local network, your phone discovers the Desktop sync host automatically to merge reading progress.

Offline .json.gz Backups

Export your complete SQLite library as a compressed .json.gz archive and restore it on any device offline.

AniList Smart Tracking

Synchronize your anime and manga progress to AniList automatically as you finish chapters and episodes.

  • Automated Scrobbling: Progress updates immediately when completing an episode or chapter.
  • Smart Completion: Automatically transitions status from CURRENT to COMPLETED on the final entry.
  • Offline SQLite Retry Queue: Updates made offline queue safely in SQLite and replay when connectivity returns.

Offline Reading & CBZ Storage

Your library, chapters, and history live strictly in local SQLite storage. Download chapters for offline trips anytime.

Bulk Downloading & CBZ Export

Queue chapters into the background downloader and store them as standard .cbz archives ready for offline reading or portable export.
